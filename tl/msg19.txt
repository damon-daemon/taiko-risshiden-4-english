1521-1580\nDaimyo of Oshu. 16th Ashina head. The restorer who made the Ashina, with the Date, the greatest daimyo of Oshu. Allied with the Uesugi, Takeda, Hojo and other powers, he fought the Satake of Hitachi. He retired for a time, but returned when his son Morioki died of illness.

1545?-1610?\nOshu clan chief. Lord of Shiomatsu. Survived the age by allying as the times required. When Date Masamune became head of the Date, he went to Yonezawa Castle and submitted. He then fought for Masamune in many battles. A master of the cross-bladed spear.

1561-1584\n18th Ashina head. Son of Nikaido Moriyoshi. A hostage of the Ashina, he married the widow of the head Morioki when he died suddenly, and inherited. He allied with the Uesugi and Hojo against the Satake, but amid feuds he was murdered by an attendant in Kurokawa Castle.

1552?-1585?\n14th Nihonmatsu Hatakeyama head, son of Yoshikuni. For sheltering Masamune's foe Ouchi Sadatsuna he was invaded. He surrendered and came to talk peace, then seized Masamune's father Terumune. Overtaken as he fled, he and Terumune died stabbing each other.

1536?-1589?\nAshina vassal of an Aizu Ashina branch. Lord of Inawashiro. He handed over to his heir Moritane, but on his second wife's slander tried to disinherit him, and they fought over the castle. Just before the battle for Aizu he went over to the Date and led their van at Suriagehara.

1527?-1589\nAshina vassal. Senior kinsman who ran the government, the "Ashina regent". After Moritaka died he held off those backing Date Masamune's brother Kojiro as heir and brought in Satake Yoshishige's second son Yoshihiro as head. He died at Suriagehara.

1548-1635\n16th Soma head. Heir of Moritane. Starting with a battle against Date Terumune, he fought the Date for over a decade. At Sekigahara he was slow to join the East and was stripped of his lands, but Date Masamune interceded and he got them back.
@197
1569-?\nAshina vassal. Son of Mimasaka. Known as Shogen. One of the Four Ashina Elders. At Suriagehara, as other elders turned traitor, he led the van and fought hard. He once reached the Date headquarters, but as his side collapsed he fled to Hitachi with his lord Morishige.

1547-1575\n17th Ashina head. Heir of Moriuji. Inherited when his father retired. Much was hoped of him, but he died at 29 during the war with the Satake. Having no son, his old father Moriuji took charge again and an heir was brought in from the Nikaido.
