●Outline\nSell rice at a rice dealer. The higher the price you get, the more merit.\n◆Procedure\nFirst check [Market Prices] to find a town where the price is high. For 100 kan they will also tell you nearby towns' prices.\nWhen you have chosen a rice dealer, choose [Sell Rice] there. With Accounting 1 or more, you can haggle over the price.\nFinally, return to the castle and hand in the money.
@3
◆One step further\nPut the money from [Sell Rice] to work and grow it. While on a [Sell Rice] mission you can also [Buy Rice]. Use this to buy cheap rice in one town with the money from the sale, and sell it where the price is high. But buying costs more than selling, so without haggling it is hard to make a profit.\nOr use the money to buy trade goods at a guild and sell them where prices are high for a profit.
