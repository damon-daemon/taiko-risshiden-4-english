1540?-1610\nIzumo daimyō, heir of Haruhisa, lord of Gassan-Toda Castle. Inheriting on his father's sudden death, he held the castle against Mōri Motonari, but lost many retainers to Motonari's divisive schemes and gave it up. He became a monk and lived on under Mōri protection.

1529-1595\nAmago vassal, lord of Setoyama in Izumo, third son of Mitsukiyo. His father was shot dead fighting Ōuchi Yoshitaka. Moved by that heroic death, Haruhisa increased the fief of Morikiyo, who inherited. But he gave up on the declining Amago and submitted to Mōri Motonari.

1516?-1591\nAmago, later Mōri vassal; lord of Mitoya Castle. He served the Ōuchi for a time, then returned. When the Mōri invaded Izumo he secretly went over to them. Going to Kyōto with Mōri Terumoto, he met Ieyasu; Terumoto grew suspicious and banished him from Izumo.

1511?-1566\nAmago vassal, lord of Uyama Castle; chief elder to Haruhisa and Yoshihisa. In the siege of Gassan-Toda he spent his own fortune on food for the garrison. Through Mōri Motonari's divisive schemes he was later suspected of dealing with the Mōri and was killed by Yoshihisa.

1529-1578\nTanba chieftain, second son of Tokiie, lord of Kuroi Castle; known as Akuemon. A brave general called the "Red Demon of Tanba". Guardian of his young nephew Tadaie, he beat back Mitsuhide's Tanba army again and again. When his castle fell he gave the foe his sable pelt.

1535?-1579?\nTanba chieftain, lord of Saikusho Castle. Known for his bravery, he fought Akechi Mitsuhide's invading army many times. After the Hatano fell, Mitsuhide invited him to serve, but he refused and sent his son Ujikiyo instead. Ujikiyo later died at Yamazaki.

1540?-1579\nTanba chieftain, son of Harumichi, brother of Hideharu. He held Yakami Castle in Tanba with his brother against Akechi Mitsuhide's campaign. After over a year of siege he surrendered, and was sent with his brother to Azuchi and crucified.

?-1582\nHatakeyama, later Oda vassal, son of Tsugumune. He fled when his grandfather Fusasada was executed by Hatakeyama Yoshitsuna, and returned when Yoshitsuna was ousted. He served Uesugi Kenshin, then the Oda. After Honnō-ji he rose to win back his lands, and died.
